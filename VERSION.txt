Kraken Derivatives Perpetual Scanner — 2.2

Rules:
- BTC SQZMOM three-closed-bar strict peak above zero activates SELL phase.
- BTC SQZMOM three-closed-bar strict trough below zero activates BUY phase.
- The BTC phase persists from peak to trough (SELL), and from trough to peak (BUY).
- Altcoin signals require a matching three-bar SQZMOM pivot, matching BTC phase, and matching active colored SMC Order Block confirmation.
- BTC itself is exempt from SMC and can alert on its newly confirmed three-bar SQZMOM pivot.
- Reversal software, ratios, and calculations are not used.
- Kraken Derivatives perpetual contracts only; 30-minute closed candles.
